# Focused source evidence follow-up (2026-10-23)

This addendum examines one high-risk Douyu recording and all six Douyu rows whose previously collected page title carries a date one day after the archive entry date. It uses the public page metadata already captured in `other-host-sources.csv` and existing source notes, plus a small number of public web searches. It does not modify the archive data or code. This follow-up did not play or download any video; a metadata page is not evidence that the video currently plays or is complete.

The seven source-level cases are listed in [`recent-source-evidence-followup.csv`](recent-source-evidence-followup.csv). “Previously collected” distinguishes the stored page metadata and source notes from pages newly inspected during this follow-up.

## 2017-08-01: high risk that the Douyu item is a clip

The Douyu page for [`x0zZVvPKRx4WO4E2`](https://v.douyu.com/show/x0zZVvPKRx4WO4E2) has previously collected title metadata `【直播时刻】 大周MC第六天：有没有邻居？ 20170801 15点场` and a page duration of 182.04 seconds. The archive currently labels this source `replay`; the archive entry itself is 240 minutes long.

The title’s “直播时刻” label and three-minute page length are a strong clip risk. This interpretation is supported by Douyu’s own [video-station announcement](https://www.douyu.com/cms/detail/6777.shtml), which distinguishes full live replays from highlights and live fragments, and describes the latter as condensed moments that can turn a two-hour stream into a three-minute video. That announcement describes platform categories in general; it does not prove the exact content of this specific item. The source page was not played during this follow-up, so the item remains a **likely clip / full replay unverified**, not a confirmed missing recording.

The entry already lists other candidate coverage: two official Youku uploads whose existing archive notes record durations 91:54 and 91:55, and an edited MC-only version whose note records about 184 minutes. Those notes are earlier archive evidence; this follow-up did not revisit those pages or inspect their content. They make the short Douyu item unnecessary as sole coverage, but do not by themselves prove the live archive is complete.

## Six one-day title/date differences

Three rows belong to `2019-08-10-live-01`: the three Douyu titles are P1/P2/P3 and each explicitly says `2019.8.11`. The P1/P2/P3 order is compatible with an overnight sequence, but no independent start/end timestamp or contemporaneous schedule was found. All three remain **unresolved date differences**; neither the entry date nor the title date should be changed from this evidence alone.

The Douyu source for `2021-04-29-live-01` says `P1 2021.4.30 PS发布会`, while the archive date is Apr 29. PlayStation’s [Apr 29, 2021 State of Play page](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/04/29/) confirms an event on that calendar date, but it does not identify this particular Douyu stream or explain its title date. The mismatch remains **unresolved**; an event-date match cannot be assumed from “PS发布会” alone.

For `2022-11-16-live-01`, the Nov 17 `00点场` page is listed in the existing archive note as segment 3/3, after segments labeled Nov 16 `20点场` and `22点场`. This sequence supports grouping the set under the Nov 16 session start, with the final part crossing midnight. It is **likely a correct cross-midnight grouping**, not evidence of a one-day archive error. These page titles and prior part notes were not revalidated by playback during this follow-up.

For `2022-12-31-live-01`, the Douyu source title says Jan 1 `01点场`. An existing note for a separate six-part Bilibili recording records capture-time titles from Dec 31 23:25 through Jan 1 01:49. This independently supports treating the archive entry as a Dec 31 cross-midnight stream grouping. It does not establish that either upload covers the entire live session. Status: **likely cross-midnight grouping; completeness unverified**.
