# AcFun source metadata audit (2026-10-23)

## Scope and result

This read-only pass used the deployed-baseline checkout at commit `34b35960` and scanned every AcFun `https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac…` source cited from `data/entries/`. It found **2,390 source citations referring to 2,389 normalized post IDs**. One post, `ac4166820`, is cited by two entries. No citation uses a page-number suffix or query parameter, so the references point to post pages rather than particular `videoList` pages.

The audit has a final page-metadata record for all 2,389 IDs: **2,387 `metadata_ok` page reads and 2 `imported_partial_metadata` records**. The final UID-enabled run fetched the 1,227 posts that had not yet been read, one GET per ID; the 1,160 successful metadata rows already in the compact journal were reused without another GET. One earlier short batch used a separate cache filename before correction and repeated 36 GETs for IDs already in the journal; it added only four previously unvisited posts. The duplicate results were not used to replace existing records, and all later requests skipped IDs already present in the canonical journal. Thus the final evidence covers 2,387 distinct posts, with 36 repeated public-page GETs during the intermediate misrouted batch. The two imported partial records are `ac2434647` and `ac2731879`: they are retained as prior partial observations, not represented as new, complete page reads. No page returned a blocking or parsing/network error. Page reads did not test playback or watch video content.

The full citation table, including entry title, duration, game labels, source `parts`, source URL, page metadata title, and UID comparison, is [acfun-references.csv](acfun-references.csv). Unique post-level metadata and each current `videoList` item are in [acfun-posts.csv](acfun-posts.csv). These two tables let reviewers inspect every citation and every distinct AcFun post.

## Account attribution

AcFun page JSON exposed a public `user.id` for the 1,227 newly fetched posts. All **1,227/1,227** matched the UID for the citation's `source.account` in `data/accounts.yaml`; there were **zero observed UID mismatches**. The 1,160 older cached successes and the two imported partial records did not include a UID and were not fetched again, so their citations remain unverified at the UID level. Four of those citations have no `source.account` field in the baseline, so even a UID-bearing page could not be checked against a declared account without changing source data. A display-name difference alone was not treated as an attribution error.

## Existing timeline snapshots

The baseline contains 14 AcFun `basis` posts with 33 snapshotted page references. Their page IDs matched the corresponding current `videoList` item IDs at every page index. Durations matched within 0.5 seconds; the small raw differences are consistent with the snapshot retaining fractional seconds while the current page metadata is rounded to whole seconds. This is a metadata-to-snapshot comparison only; it does not establish that the video content is complete or that all AcFun posts have correct part order. See [acfun-timeline-basis.csv](acfun-timeline-basis.csv).

The two existing `part_details` entries for `ac4166820` cover pages 1–9 across two citations. All nine saved titles and durations matched the current `videoList` item at the same array page index; see [acfun-part-details-check.csv](acfun-part-details-check.csv). Existing timeline snapshots and `part_details` therefore agree with the current page metadata in the checked cases. They do not validate unsnapshotted posts.

## Review candidates

The machine-screened candidate table is [acfun-review-candidates.csv](acfun-review-candidates.csv). Each row is a prompt for human review, not a finding that footage is missing or misclassified.

- **One `source.parts` versus page count discrepancy:** entry `2017-01-21-live-01` cites [`ac3422592`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac3422592) with `parts: 1`, while its current page lists four `videoList` items. The post title indicates “part1”; the page also contains multiple items. This may be a source-field convention or a stale count and needs contextual review.
- **Ten posts contain one or more page items whose reported duration is zero.** The affected post IDs and item titles are in the candidate CSV. Zero is the page metadata value, not evidence that the item has no footage.
- **Twenty-seven posts have non-monotonic part numbers in their current page titles.** For example, [`ac3160298`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac3160298) lists titles P2, P3, P4, then P1 in `videoList` order. Navigation follows the current array order; the title numbers are only a review cue. The complete candidate sequences are included in the CSV. A non-monotonic label sequence does not by itself mean a part is absent or that pages should be reordered.
- **Eighty-eight entry records (116 cited unique-post rows) have a large difference between `duration_min` and the sum of the distinct AcFun post item durations** using a screening threshold of greater than 30 minutes or 50% of the entry duration, whichever is larger. This is deliberately broad: an entry can have game-only duration, source clips can overlap or cover only part of the stream, and platform metadata may not represent playable duration. These are duration-semantics candidates only and should not be interpreted as proof of truncation.

I also scanned all 52 AcFun citations with an existing `source.note`. Seven notes contain explicit partial-source, title/game conflict, or unusually short-duration clues. These are **pre-existing archive notes**, not findings from this page-metadata pass: [`ac1736364`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac1736364) is described as “直播部分”; [`ac2010055`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac2010055) is described as a three-minute live clip with date assignment noted as inferred; [`ac2163929`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac2163929) is described as a three-part “全程录像” with historical cross-source checks; [`ac2353998`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac2353998) is explicitly described as a short edited segment from a longer entry; [`ac2553531`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac2553531) has a title/game conflict noted from earlier content review; [`ac2662778`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac2662778) has an implausible title date noted; and [`ac2678564`](https://www.acfun.cn/v/ac2678564) is noted as shorter than the entry's session duration. Their earlier notes are evidence to preserve, but this audit did not replay or independently confirm those content claims.

No automated title-similarity score was used to declare a source title wrong; the post metadata title and entry title are both available in the full references CSV for human comparison. Title agreement cannot verify the video contents.

## Reproduction and limits

`audit_acfun.py` parses source URLs, normalizes duplicate IDs, reads only the public post page, extracts title/owner/date/page video IDs, titles, durations, upload times, public UID when exposed, and writes per-reference and per-post CSVs. It never follows media or stream URLs. `summarize_acfun.py` joins the saved metadata to a selected baseline and emits the review-candidate CSV. The cache is a compact JSONL journal on the user-designated external storage; no media was downloaded or stored. No files under `data/` or production data were changed.

The audit confirms public-page metadata and source-account UID matches only for freshly fetched pages. It does **not** confirm each item's actual playback length, whether the recording begins at the start of the broadcast, whether it ends at the true end, whether parts contain blackouts or skips, or whether all relevant game segments are represented. The 1,160 earlier cached successes were reused with their recorded fetch times and were not re-fetched merely to obtain UIDs; their UID attribution remains unconfirmed. The two imported partial observations are explicitly separate from complete page-metadata successes. None of these metadata records establishes continuous availability or current playback. Page titles, post duration fields, and all-P labels are not substitutes for content inspection.
